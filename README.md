## Why this exists

AI is full of words that sound complicated: *token*, *hallucination*, *RAG*, *agent*. Most explanations assume you already know the basics, or skip the basics entirely.

This dictionary starts from zero and goes as deep as you need. Each term is explained in everyday language, with a real example, and a note on what it is easy to confuse with.
